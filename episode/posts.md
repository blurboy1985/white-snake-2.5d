## LinkedIn

I made a 70-second animated short of a 400-year-old Chinese legend, built almost entirely from code.

The Legend of the White Snake: a snake spirit, a scholar with an umbrella, a meddling monk, and a pagoda that really did collapse in 1924.

How it was made:
• Script and sources written with Claude, every claim checked against a source
• Characters drawn as code (SVG), sets built by Python scripts in Blender
• Narration, music and sound effects from ElevenLabs
• Each camera move and expression change is timed to a spoken word, so changing the voiceover re-times the whole film

Source code: https://github.com/blurboy1985/white-snake-2.5d
